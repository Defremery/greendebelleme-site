An estate in the Perche

Le Green de Bellême occupies the former outbuildings of the golf estate: the Longère and its tower, the Sablons courtyard, the Haut-Val houses. Honey-coloured stone, timber frames, views of Bellême's old town. The 18-hole course starts at your door; Bellême state forest, one of France's finest oak woods, is minutes away.

Frequently asked questions

What time is check-in?

Self check-in from 3 pm to 8 pm, check-out before 11 am. The day before, you receive your unit number and access code; the estate map guides you to the door.

What if I arrive late?

No problem: check-in is self-service with a code. Just let us know on +33 2 33 85 68 82 if you arrive after 8 pm.

Check-out time?

Check-out before 11 am. Late check-out is possible when available for bookings made on our site.

Are pets allowed?

Yes in the apartments (Fairway, Écuyers, Percheron), with a €15 per day supplement. Pets are not accepted in the Longère rooms.

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking by the buildings on the estate.

Breakfast and restaurants?

Apartments have a fitted kitchen; tea and coffee are provided. On the estate, the golf brasserie (+33 2 44 51 39 78); Bellême, 5 minutes away, has bakeries and good restaurants.

What about golf?

The 18-hole course starts at your door. Our guests get a preferential rate from 6 green fees; we also put together golf breaks (nights + green fees).

Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi is included in every stay.
